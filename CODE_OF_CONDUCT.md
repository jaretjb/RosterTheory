# Code of conduct

We want a useful, welcoming place to discuss fantasy-football software. Be
respectful of different experience levels, league formats, and viewpoints.
Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
language, repeated unwanted contact, and publishing someone else's private
league data or credentials are not acceptable.

These expectations apply in repository issues, discussions, reviews, and
other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn a
participant, or limit participation. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate to the behavior and documented privately where appropriate.

Report conduct concerns through a private channel offered by a maintainer. If
none is listed, open a minimal issue requesting one without repeating harmful
content or personal information. The maintainer will review reports as
capacity permits and avoid retaliating against good-faith reporters.
